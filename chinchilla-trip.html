The Chinchilla's Big Trip

龙猫飞北京 · a fairy-tale math journey

Once upon a time, a little gray chinchilla lived in Shanghai. He missed his best friend in Beijing so much. One night, he quietly climbed onto an airplane…
But the chinchilla can't understand people! Help him solve English math problems to get his ticket, drink, eat, use the toilet, and take a taxi home.
